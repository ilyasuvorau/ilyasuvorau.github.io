Failure Files Analytics

A small private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Failure Files to read the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources) and send a daily summary to the owner.

It is not a public service. It only ever signs in to the owner's own channel and has read-only access: it cannot upload, edit, delete or publish anything on YouTube.
